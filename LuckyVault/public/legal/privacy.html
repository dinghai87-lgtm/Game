Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Operator: [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS] · Contact: support@example.com

Summary. We collect what we need to run your account and the games, keep them fair and secure, and (if you agree) send you notifications. We do not sell your personal information.

1. What we collect

  • Account: username, password (stored only as a salted hash), and optionally your email address and phone number. If you sign in with Apple or Google we receive an account identifier and, if shared, your email.
  • Gameplay: games played, bets and wins in virtual coins, levels, missions, achievements, leaderboard scores and time spent in games.
  • Purchases: order number, package, price, status and date. Card details are handled by the app store or payment provider and never reach us.
  • Device and security: a random device identifier created by the app, IP address, and basic browser/app information, used to prevent abuse such as multiple accounts collecting bonuses.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a push subscription token for your device.

2. How we use it

  • To provide your account, coins and games and to process purchases.
  • To send verification and password-reset codes.
  • To keep the service fair and secure, and enforce our Terms.
  • To apply your spending limit and responsible-play settings.
  • To improve the games using aggregated statistics (for example how many players use a game).
  • To send in-app messages and, if you agree, push notifications. You can turn push off at any time in Account.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers who run parts of the service for us (hosting, database, email and SMS delivery, app stores and payment processing), under contracts that protect it, or when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Retention

We keep account data while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your email, phone number, sign-in links and password and close the account; limited purchase and audit records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Act, the GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or object to some uses. Use Account → Delete account to delete it yourself, or email support@example.com.

6. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to the back office is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Age

Lucky Vault is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our servers may be located outside your country. Where required we use appropriate safeguards for transfers.

9. Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes and tell you in the app about important changes.
