Effective 22 November 2026. This page is the company-level refund policy for digital goods published by ELERONZEN TECHNOLOGY INC. Game-specific terms for Hero Defense are also in the Terms of Service.

What we sell

Hero Defense is a free tower-defense game. Optional purchases, where offered on the web version, are digital goods only (in-game virtual items such as gems, gold, heroes, gear or skins). They are licensed for use in the game, have no cash value, and cannot be exchanged for money, physical goods or transferred to another player. We do not sell physical products on this website.

When we refund

Digital goods are delivered instantly after payment is confirmed, so sales are generally final once the items have been delivered to your account. We will refund a purchase, or restore the items, when:

  • you were charged more than once for the same order;
  • a payment was made without your authorization;
  • you were charged but the items were not delivered because of a fault on our side;
  • a refund is otherwise required by consumer-protection law where you live.

Web purchases are processed by Stripe. We never receive your card number. Approved refunds for web purchases go back to the original payment method through Stripe; how long that takes depends on your bank or card issuer. In-app purchases on iOS or Android, when they become available, are processed by the App Store or Google Play and follow that platform’s refund policy.

How to request a refund

Email info@eleronzentechnology.com. Include the account or order reference, the email used at checkout, and a short description of the problem. If you were charged but did not receive your items, please write as soon as you can.
